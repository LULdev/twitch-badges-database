Details

Consecutive wins within a day build a combo that multiplies game XP, for example a three-win run giving 1.5x, resetting on a loss or at the daily reset and computed server-side from the member's round outcomes. It appears as a small combo meter in the arcade and writes a feed entry when it breaks a personal record, while the daily game-XP cap still bounds the total. Effort: M. Risk if built badly: it amplifies variance and rewards the highest-variance game, so the combo must be capped low and applied only to win XP, never to coins. Why: It adds a skill-flavoured reward loop to an arcade that currently pays every win the same. Source: new-ideas-10-xp-coin-admin.json

Mid-Round Cash-Out

Details

In the games with a running multiplier, such as tower, the coin-flip ladder and higher-or-lower, the player gets a server-enforced settle-now action that banks the current value instead of continuing, recorded with the round outcome in game_rounds. It reads the same server-authoritative round state those games already keep. Effort: M. Risk if built badly: a race between the settle call and the next round step lets a player bank after seeing the next result, so the decision must be resolved inside the single server round transition. Why: It turns pure nerve into a real decision in the risk games, which is what makes them replayable. Source: new-ideas-10-xp-coin-admin.json

Details

Cohort Notification Segments

Details

An admin can send an in-app notification and optionally a push to a filter-selected cohort, such as members at level 1 who never played, members inactive for 30 days, or holders of one achievement, built from user_progress filters and delivered through the existing notification and push paths.
