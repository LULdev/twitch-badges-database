It is opt-in, stored with their other settings, and displayed like a budget. Effort: S. Risk if built badly: it becomes nagware members resent, so it must never be applied silently and must be easy to start, hard to switch off on impulse. Why: A real coin economy on a gambling-flavoured arcade owes its players a way to stop. Source: new-ideas-10-xp-coin-admin.json
